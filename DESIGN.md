## Brand & Style
The design system draws inspiration from historic Indian tea estates of Darjeeling, Assam, and the Nilgiris, merged with modern artisanal luxury. The aesthetic evokes the quiet majesty of dawn mist over terrace plantations, warmth of the morning sun, and refined craftsmanship. It caters to discerning connoisseurs, gift buyers, and daily ritualists seeking provenance, terroir transparency, and an elevated sensory experience.

The design movement blends **Warm Editorial Heritage** with **Artisanal Minimalism**:
- Uncluttered, generous linen backdrops that breathe tranquility.
- Regal estate greens rooted in earthiness, paired with sunlit golden ochre and gold-leaf micro-accents.
- High-contrast editorial serif headings reminiscent of antique botanical catalogs and estate seals, balanced by hyper-legible, warm contemporary sans-serif typography for digital clarity.
- Delicate botanical framing, hairline filigree dividers, and tactile deckled-edge sensibilities applied to a seamless modern commerce flow.

## Colors
The palette is derived directly from the tea ceremony ritual and the brand insignia:
- **Primary (`#1B4332`)**: Deep Forest Estate Green. Serves as the bedrock for brand identity, prime actions, high-emphasis headings, and rich containers.
- **Secondary (`#D49B35`)**: Warm Sun Amber / Harvest Ochre. Symbolizes early morning light, golden liquor infusions, and refined gold-leaf accents. Used for badges, highlights, price accents, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#0D2818`)**: Night Shade Pine. Used for high-contrast deep footer surfaces, dark interactive hover states, and critical text emphasis.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2`)**: Warm Linen Ecru. Serves as the primary viewport surface, banishing clinical pure whites in favor of tactile paper parchment. Paired with `#F4EFE6` for tiered sub-surfaces and cards, and `#E8E0D2` for delicate hairline borders.
- **Text Tiers**: Headings and titles sit in `#0D2818` or `#1B4332`; secondary descriptive copy rests in `#40534C`; muted metadata in `#708075`.

## Typography
The typographic system establishes a graceful rhythm between classic high-fashion editorial presence and modern digital utility.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display)**: Reserved for hero statements, chapter marks, product family names, and story-driven quotes. The italic variant should be deployed sparingly for tasting notes (e.g., *Muscatel notes with honeyed undertones*).
- **Refined Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Applied to specifications, flush dates, harvest batches, ingredient percentages, and interactive inputs to ensure maximum legibility at glance speed.
- **Label Caps Tracking**: All uppercase small labels (like estate codes, flush indicators, and tags) use `0.15em` wide letter spacing to emulate heritage British-Indian apothecary and tea crate stencils.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects the spacious cadence of high-end lifestyle publications and estate archives:

- **Grid Architecture**: Standard 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`max-w-7xl` centered) with 24px gutters and 32px canvas padding. On mobile, reflows to a clean 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins.
- **Negative Space Philosophy**: Sections are generously separated by `space-xl` (40px) or `3.5rem` (56px) on desktop to establish an unhurried, luxurious browsing tempo.
- **Editorial Asymmetry**: Storytelling modules (Estate origin, leaf grading guides, tasting notes) use 7/5 or 8/4 column splits to contrast large botanical imagery against structured tasting parameters.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews heavy synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Paper Layering** and **Ambient Sunlit Diffusions**:

- **Tonal Stepping**: Depth is communicated by resting `#F4EFE6` (tinted warm card) on `#FAF7F2` (ground linen), highlighted by subtle 1px inner borders (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)` or `#E8E0D2`).
- **Warm Golden Ambient Shadows**: When elevation is required for floating selectors, mega menus, or modals, shadows are soft, diffuse, and warm-tinted:
  - Default Card Hover: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(212, 155, 53, 0.05)`.
  - Drawer & Modal Elevation: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(13, 40, 24, 0.16)`.
- **Gilded Filigree Accents**: Delicate 1px borders in metallic amber (`rgba(212, 155, 53, 0.35)`) signify certified harvest seals, limited reserves, and active filter states.

## Shapes
A restrained, soft architectural shape language (`roundedness: 1`) provides structure and formality:
- Base elements (buttons, text inputs, standard cards) carry a tailored 4px radius (`rounded-sm`), mirroring the crisp edges of handcrafted tea packaging and embossed paper labels.
- Pill tags (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for regional badges (e.g., "Assam Valley", "First Flush 2026") and circular estate seals.
- Imagery containers utilize straight or softly chamfered corners, occasionally paired with arched top edges (`rounded-t-[80px]`) for hero brand storytelling vignettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Estate Green)**: Solid `#1B4332` fill, `#FAF7F2` text, 4px corner radius. High-tracking label with subtle micro-transition to `#0D2818` on hover.
- **Secondary (Sun Amber Gilding)**: Outlined button with 1.5px border in `#D49B35`, text in `#1B4332`. Fills softly with `rgba(212, 155, 53, 0.08)` on hover.
- **Tertiary (Ghost Ritual)**: Borderless, `#1B4332` text accompanied by a fine bottom hairline underline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Cards (Tea Tin & Caddy Showcases)
- Crafted on `#F4EFE6` with a delicate 1px border of `#E8E0D2`.
- Content hierarchy: Small uppercase origin stamp (`label-caps` in `#D49B35`), followed by `headline-sm` in `#1B4332`, tasting notes tag row, and bottom price tier.
- Hover transforms card with a gentle -2px vertical lift and the activation of a faint `#D49B35` border halo.

### Chips & Origin Badges
- Filter tags for Flush, Elevation, and Processing Type (e.g., "Whole Leaf Orthodx", "Silver Needle").
- Inactive: `#FAF7F2` background, 1px `#D9D2C5` border, `#40534C` text.
- Selected: `#1B4332` solid fill with `#FAF7F2` text, adorned with a tiny sunburst glyph or dot indicator.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat linen background (`#FFFFFF` with `#FAF7F2` inset), crisp 1px `#D8D0C2` perimeter.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1B4332` with a 2px outer ring glow of `rgba(212, 155, 53, 0.25)`.
- Labels float in `label-sm` font in `#1B4332`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom square with 2px radius. Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#708075`.
- Checked: Solid `#1B4332` fill featuring a golden ochre checkmark (`#D49B35`).

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Infusion Meter**: Sensory gauge showing Steep Time (minutes), Water Temp (85°-95°C), and Caffeine Level rendered with amber pipette nodes.
- **Tasting Wheel Strip**: Horizontal aroma descriptor badges (e.g., "Malted Caramel", "Wet Orchid", "Smoked Pine") with tonal opacity fills.
- **Terroir & Elevation Stamp**: Seal displaying estate longitude, latitude, and meter elevation (e.g., "6,200 FT • Darjeeling") encapsulated in a thin golden circular border.
